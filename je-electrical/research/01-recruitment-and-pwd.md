**Part A: General English (Q1–50), from the booklet:** fill in the blanks (Q1–10), synonyms (11–20), antonyms (21–30), idioms and phrases (31–40), collective nouns (41–50).

**Part B: Electrical Engineering (Q51–150), topic list read from the booklet:**
- **Circuits and fields (Q51–67):** KCL, Wheatstone bridge, Norton, capacitors, hysteresis loop, inductance, peak factor, parallel resonance, power triangle and p.f., RL power calculation, 3-phase star/delta conversion.
- **Measurements (Q68–72):** PMMC scale, electrostatic voltmeter for HVDC, ohmmeter, resistance measurement, induction wattmeter.
- **DC machines (Q73–76):** armature resistance, welding generator, DC series motor torque.
- **Transformers (Q77–85):** mutual flux, no-load current components, leakage flux, cooling of small transformers, SC test, autotransformer, maximum efficiency.
- **Induction motors (Q83–93):** synchronous speed, rotor frequency and slip, torque calculation, starting inrush, split-phase and capacitor motors, skewing.
- **Synchronous machines (Q94–96):** prime-mover rating, parallel operation, under-excited p.f.
- **Generation and economics (Q97–100):** plant capacity from load and capacity factors, India's generation mix, economic voltage (Kelvin's law), economic p.f.
- **Transmission and distribution (Q101–118):** conductor size versus voltage, dielectric strength of air, distributor design, suspension insulators, string efficiency, corona, voltage regulation, feeder/distributor/service main, cables above 66 kV, radial system, cable stress, booster, per-unit reactance change of base, reactors.
- **Faults and protection (Q119–129):** zero-sequence current, fault-prone parts, CB contacts, current chopping (vacuum/SF6), relay CT supply, fusing factor, restriking voltage, Merz-Price protection, relay burden, lightning protection.
- **Control systems (Q130–135):** zero state, asymptotic stability, Routh-Hurwitz, gain margin.
- **Electronics (Q136–145):** semiconductor resistivity, rectifier efficiency, centre-tap transformer, negative feedback, oscillators (Wien, Colpitts), Boolean absorption law, sequential circuits.
- **Power electronics (Q146–150):** SCR forward blocking, gate pulse, choppers, AC voltage controller, pulse gating.

Source: the question booklet linked above. Topic grouping is mine; question numbers are from the booklet.

The booklet has no questions on building-wiring codes, estimation or SOR. No Meghalaya or general-knowledge questions were set either, because there was no GK paper.

## Open questions

1. **Interview date:** the notice says 12 Nov 2026, but the candidate believes 11 Nov. Check the downloaded intimation letter and the PI-notice page (https://mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in/notifications-pi.html) for any corrigendum.
2. **Document list:** the exact list will be in the intimation letter, which is behind the candidate login. Look in particular for whether an NOC or experience certificate is asked for. As a MeECL contractual employee, the candidate should also ask MPSC whether an NOC is needed; the advertisement is silent.
3. **Interview maximum marks** for this post are not published anywhere I found. Recent MPSC engineering interviews awarded about 21–36.
4. **Why 11 candidates were called for 1 post** when the policy is 1:10. A tie at the cut-off is possible but unconfirmed.
5. **Age relaxation:** the "no age limit" clause covers "Meghalaya Govt. Service". Whether MeECL contractual service counts was not checked; it matters only if the candidate was over 27 (or 32 with ST relaxation) on 01-07-2021.
6. **Officers:** who currently heads PWD (Buildings), its Electrical Division in Shillong, and the Electrical Sub-Division in Tura. Directory names are undated.
7. **Assembly building:** whether it was completed or inaugurated after 27 Aug 2026.
8. **CPWD specifications:** whether Meghalaya PWD (Buildings) Electrical uses the CPWD General Specifications for Electrical Works alongside its own SOR. No source found.

# Maths lesson/example/trap verification (m01–m08)

Scope: lessons[].body, examples[].solution/shortcut, traps[], formulas[].when/why, practice 'Shortcut:' lines, plus redundancy (coordinator request). Every number in changed or new text was recomputed with python/sympy (brute-force enumeration for relations and seating counts). Replaced practice questions keep their id, level, topic and answer letter. ⏎ marks a line break inside a field.

Checked: 8 modules, 56 lessons, 64 worked examples (solutions + shortcuts), 64 traps, 168 formula when/why texts, 77 practice 'Shortcut:' lines.

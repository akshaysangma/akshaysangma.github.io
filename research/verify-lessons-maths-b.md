# Lesson/example/trap verification log — maths m09–m17

Scope: everything in modules m09–m17 except practice answer keys and the `formulas` tex already covered in verify-maths.md. That means `lessons[].body` (rules, tables, mini-examples, "recognise it" claims), `examples[].solution`/`shortcut`, `traps[]`, `formulas[].when` (no module has `why` fields), practice lines starting "Shortcut:", and redundancy, which the coordinator added to the scope. All numbers and symbolic results were recomputed with sympy (venv /tmp/lcheck2). Pre-edit backups are in /tmp/lbackup.

**Redundancy rule:** a practice item or worked example that reuses the exact numbers of a lesson mini-example or worked example (so its answer is already visible) was replaced with a variant. Each variant was verified with sympy and keeps the same id, level and topic. Items that share only structure, with different numbers, were kept. Where a lesson mini-example repeated a worked example, the lesson got new numbers. The exception is when the lesson item is the real IPO 2022 question: then the example was changed instead, so the lesson keeps the 2022 question.

**Totals checked:** 76 lessons, 72 worked examples, 71 traps, 258 formula `when` texts, 119 practice Shortcut lines.

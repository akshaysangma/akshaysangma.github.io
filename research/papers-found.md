# Meghalaya IPO (MPSC) - papers and supporting material found

Research date: 27-Sep-2026. All local files are in `research/papers/`.
"Verified" means I downloaded the document and read it (text layer or rendered page image). "Site claims" means I only saw a listing or link title and did not open the document.

Access note: `mpsc.nic.in` no longer resolves (NXDOMAIN). The current official site is `https://mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in/`, and it uses the same paths (`misc/`, `advt/`, `notify/`, `results/`). Older `mpsc.nic.in` pages and files were recovered through the Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org`).

## 1. Headline findings

1. **Only one IPO question paper is published anywhere I could reach: IPO 2022 (26-Nov-2022).** No other IPO paper exists on the MPSC site, the MPSC PYQ page, megeducations.com (full media library listed via its WP API) or meghalayajobportal.in.
2. **The file you already have is the official paper.** The megeducations PDF is byte-identical to the MPSC copy at `https://mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in/misc/26112022IPO22.pdf` (same MD5 `fc089719504a5ee312c4b03a4ef4a18f`, 7,293,550 bytes). Verified.
3. **IPO recruitment history (verified from MPSC documents):**
   - **2012 cycle:** Advt No. MPSC/ADVT-38/1/2012-2013/1 dated 20-Apr-2012. Vacancies went from 22 to 23 (corrigendum, 4-May-2012), then to 31 (addendum, 18-Apr-2013). A **written examination was held on 16-Nov-2013**. The result was published 28-Mar-2014 (`results/28mar2014a.pdf`). **That 2013 paper was never put online** in any source I found, and the result PDF itself was not archived.
   - **2016 cycle:** Advt No. MPSC/ADVT-38/1/2016-2017/1 dated 26-Apr-2016, 13 vacancies. The screening test was held **26-Nov-2022** (this is the IPO-22 paper). 40 roll numbers qualified for interview, held 14-16 Feb 2023. **13 candidates were recommended** on 23-Mar-2023.
   - **2024 cycle:** Advt No. MPSC/ADVT-54/1/2023-2024/138 dated 16-Jan-2024, 30 vacancies. On 30-Jul-2025 it was postponed because the matter was before the High Court of Meghalaya. On 15-Sep-2026 a corrigendum cut vacancies to **26**. On 25-Sep-2026 MPSC confirmed the **screening test for 28-Nov-2026, 10:00 AM to 12:30 PM** (2.5 h), at MPSC Office Shillong, Shillong Law College, and Garo Union Hr. Sec. School, Tura (1,133 seats in total).
4. **Scheme conflict to flag.** The 2024 advertisement specifies **"General English & General Knowledge - 150 marks; Mathematics - 200 marks"** (total 350). The 2022 paper was GE 100 + GK 100 + Maths 150 (total 350). The 2026 exam may therefore weight Maths more heavily than 2022 (for example, 100 Maths questions instead of 75). This is inferred from the advertisement only; the booklet format is not yet published.
5. **No official answer key for IPO 2022 exists** on the MPSC answer-key page, the "notifications-ak" page, or any archived snapshot of `mpsc.nic.in` answerkeys.html (2019-2024). MPSC only started posting answer keys for recent exams.
6. **Interview weightage: not published.** No IPO document gives interview marks. The 2016 advt only says the interview-to-vacancy ratio is "limited to 1:2 to 1:5". In the 2022 cycle it was 40 called for 13 posts.

## 4. Syllabus / scheme evidence (verbatim)

**A. 2024 advertisement (governs the 28-Nov-2026 exam)**, https://mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in/advt/Advt16Jan2024.pdf, page 7:
> "9.1 (b) For post of Industrial Promotion Officer/Assistant Industries Officer/Assistant Manager,CFSW/Superintendent of Industries in Commerce & Industries Department.
> Screening Test consisting of the following subjects.
> 1) General English & General Knowledge- 150 marks
> 2) Mathematics – 200 marks.
> Only those candidates who qualifies the Screening Test will be called to appear for Personal Interview."

Page 3 (eligibility):
> "2.1(b) For the post of Industrial Promotion Officer / Assistant Industries Officer / Assistant Manager, CFSW / Superintendent of Industries in Commerce & Industries Department. Degree Holder in any branch of Engineering except Civil Engineering from a recognized University."

Page 3 (age): "For post at Sl.No 2, 4 :- Age will be calculated with reference to 01.01.2024 and should have attained the age of 18 yrs to 32yrs. Upper age limit is relaxable by 5 (five) years for SC/ST."
Page 2: pay "Level -15 as per the Revised Pay Structure"; 30 vacancies (UR 4, KJ 12, Garo 12, Other ST/SC 2). Fee Rs. 460 (half for SC/ST residents of Meghalaya). Last date 6-Feb-2024.
Page 7, 9.2: "Only those candidates who are declared to have passed the Screening Test/ Main Written Examination shall be eligible to the Personal Interview." No interview marks are given.

**B. 2016 advertisement (governed the 2022 exam)**, https://mpsc.nic.in/advt/adv26042016.pdf (text PDF):
> "10. Industrial Promotion Officer / Assistant Industries Officer / Superintendent of Industries in the Directorate of Commerce & Industries. Rs.17000-470-20290-EB-560-25330-760-33690/-pm. Graduate in any branch of Engineering (except Civil Engineering) from any Government recognized University. [Vacancies] 13. 18 to 27 years with relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST candidates."

General scheme clause (not IPO-specific):
> "Screening Tests or Preliminary Screening Tests, where decided to be held, shall be Multiple Choice OMR based Tests. Such Tests shall be held for 350 Marks, on: (i) General English – 100 Marks (of the standard of the Qualification prescribed for the post) (ii) General Awareness & Aptitude – 150 Marks (iii) Mathematics – 100 Marks (10th Standard)"
> "...the ratio of the number of candidates called for interview to the vacancies should be limited to 1:2 to 1:5."

The actual 2022 IPO paper did **not** follow this generic split. Its cover says: "Subjects : General English, General Knowledge & Mathematics ... Marks: 350, Time: 2 1/2 hours ... Part-A-General English : 50 questions, Part-B-General Knowledge : 50 questions, Part-C- Mathematics : 75 questions ... There will NOT be any negative marking."

**C. No IPO syllabus document exists.** The MPSC syllabus page (https://mpsc.meghalaya.gov.in/syllabus.html, live and archived) lists about 28 syllabi, and none is for IPO. The 2024 advertisement gives only subject names and marks. The Scribd "SchemeSyllabus for Industrial promotion officer" (scribd.com/document/559661266) is the **Andhra Pradesh** APPSC IPO syllabus, **not Meghalaya**.

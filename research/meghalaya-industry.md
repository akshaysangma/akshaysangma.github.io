## 2. Meghalaya Industrial & Investment Promotion Policy (MIIPP) 2024: the current state policy

All facts below are from the policy PDF on megindustry.gov.in, except where marked.

### 2.1 Identity
| Item | Fact |
|---|---|
| Notification | No. IND.15/2024/82, **dated Shillong, 14 March 2024**, signed by Pravin Bakshi, Commissioner & Secretary. FLAG: TeamLease RegTech reports the date as 15 Mar 2024. The PDF says 14 Mar |
| Cabinet nod | Early March 2024 (Shillong Times, 12 Mar 2024) |
| Replaces | **MIIPP 2012** (repealed by §6.3) |
| Policy period | In force **from 21 Dec 2022 to 20 Dec 2034**, plus extra years to pay committed liabilities |
| Registration window | Original: from notification to **31.03.2026**. Registration applications to be disposed of by 30.09.2026. **13 Aug 2026**: Cabinet **extended the registration period by one year**. FLAG: the 31.03.2027 end date is inferred from "one year"; the extension notification was not seen |
| "New unit" | Registered after the notification, and no later than 30.09.2026 in the original text. Must start commercial production or operation **within 4 years** of registration. Must have a **new GSTIN**; cannot be a split-up, relocated or reopened unit. A restored heritage property used for tourism or hospitality can count as new |
| "Expansion" | Extra investment of **≥ 25%** of the existing investment in plant & machinery (manufacturing) or buildings/durable assets (services) |
| Purpose | Align with and top up the central **UNNATI 2024**. Serve the CM's **"Mission 10"**: a USD 10 bn economy by 2028, 5 lakh jobs, "10 opportunities and 10 guarantees" |
| Priority sectors | FLAG: the policy lists **16 priority sectors** in a graphic that could not be extracted as text. Summaries confirm food processing and tourism are among them. Priority-sector units get higher rates and caps |
| Nodal agency (disbursal) | Department of Commerce & Industries. Registration is through the **Invest Meghalaya Authority**, now **MIPA**, on the **Unified Investment Portal (UIP)** |

### 2.2 Eligibility
- **Manufacturing**: minimum **₹1 crore** in plant & machinery (P&M).
- **Services**: minimum **₹50 lakh** in buildings and durable physical assets.
- **Micro units**: minimum **₹50 lakh**. For micro units, building cost is counted with P&M.
- **Local employment undertaking** (domiciles of Meghalaya):
  - Years 1–2: **≥ 25% of managerial** and **≥ 90% of non-managerial** posts.
  - From year 3: **≥ 50% of managerial** and 90% of non-managerial.
  - Minimum wages must be paid. The State can relax the quota if skills are unavailable locally.
- Not eligible: **Central/State PSUs**, units with more than 50% government stake, and any amount paid in cash.
- Production may be phased (up to 3 phases). Incentives flow only after final commencement.
- **Change of location** (§6.2):
  - Closing, relocating or disposing of assets **within 10 years** → no further incentives.
  - Doing so **within 5 years** → **refund everything already received**.
- Misrepresentation → refund with **15% p.a.** interest, plus criminal liability.

### 2.3 Incentives
| Incentive | Rate / cap |
|---|---|
| **State Capital Investment Incentive** (new or expanding units) | **30%** of P&M (manufacturing) or buildings/durable assets (services). **MSME caps**: ₹10 cr (priority sector) / ₹8 cr (other). **Large units**: ₹15 cr (priority) / ₹12 cr (non-priority). Only the difference above the UNNATI amount is paid |
| **State Interest Subvention** | **5% p.a. (priority)** or **4% p.a. (non-priority)**, for up to **7 consecutive years**. Loans up to **₹250 cr** principal, from scheduled commercial banks or RBI-registered FIs. Paid only after commercial production starts |
| **SGST reimbursement** (new units only) | **100% of net SGST** (SGST paid minus ITC). **Priority**: up to **15 yrs**, capped at **150%** of eligible investment. **Non-priority**: up to **10 yrs**, capped at **100%**. Paid at 1/10th of the entitlement per year. SGST on exports is excluded |
| Additional capital incentive for physically challenged / special-category owners (Meghalaya domicile, majority shareholder) | **Micro +10%**; **Small +5%** (cap ₹50 lakh) |
| **Transport subsidy** (Micro & Small; raw materials sourced in Meghalaya, finished goods made in Meghalaya) | **₹15 lakh/yr for 4 yrs (priority)**; **₹10 lakh/yr for 3 yrs (non-priority)** |
| **Green subsidies** | Rainwater harvesting 50%: cap ₹2 lakh (MSE) / ₹10 lakh (M&L). Wastewater recycling 50%: cap ₹5 lakh (Medium) / ₹15 lakh (Large). Water audit 75% reimbursement up to ₹1 lakh, if it saves ≥ 25% water. Zero-discharge facility 50%: cap ₹5 lakh (Medium) / ₹10 lakh (Large) |
| **Power** | DG / renewable captive sets 50%: MSE cap ₹10 lakh (conventional) / ₹25 lakh (renewable); Medium & Large cap ₹50 lakh / ₹1 cr. Only one claim per unit; renewable units must be on net metering. 11 kV+ line drawl: 100% up to ₹10 lakh (MSE). Service connection charges: 50% up to ₹2 lakh (MSE) |
| **EPF reimbursement** | **100% of employer's EPF contribution for 3 years**, plus **1 more year for women employees**, for Meghalaya domiciles. Cap: 12% of basic + DA, or **₹1,800/month**, whichever is lower. Not for IT/ITeS units, which are covered by the **Meghalaya IT/ITeS Policy 2024** |
| **Customised package** | Investment **≥ ₹100 cr**, or green-technology industries with registered patents at any investment size. Decided by the High-Powered Committee |
| Procurement preference | Under the **Meghalaya Procurement Preference Policy for MSEs, 2020** |
| **Overall cap** | **₹100 cr per unit** from all components (can be exceeded under a customised package) |
| Overlaps | Claim central incentives (UNNATI etc.) first; the State pays only the balance. Units under **CM-ELEVATE** get only the SGST incentive from MIIPP, but can still use UNNATI |

### 6.2 NEIDS 2017 (North East Industrial Development Scheme): predecessor to UNNATI
- Effective **1 Apr 2017 to 31 Mar 2022**. Outlay **₹3,000 cr**. Covered all **8 NE states** including Sikkim.
- Eligible: new manufacturing and service units, including biotech and hydel units up to 10 MW.
- Components:
  - **Central Capital Investment Incentive for Access to Credit (CCIIAC)**: 30% of P&M.
  - Plus interest, insurance, income-tax and GST reimbursement, transport and employment incentives.
- Max **₹200 cr per unit**. **NEDFi** was the disbursing nodal agency.
- FLAG: the NEIDS component caps (e.g. ₹5 cr for CCIIAC) were not re-checked in this session.

### 6.3 NEDFi (North Eastern Development Finance Corporation Ltd)
- Incorporated under the Companies Act 1956 on **9 Aug 1995**. HQ **Guwahati** (Dispur). **RBI-registered NBFC since 2002**.
- Finances MSMEs, large, infrastructure and agri-allied projects in the NER.
- Runs the NEDFi Micro Finance Scheme (via MFIs) and the Micro-Lending Scheme (direct lending). Over 90% of micro-finance beneficiaries are women.
- Runs the DoNER **Atmanirbhar Hastshilpkar Scheme** for artisans (2021).
- Maintains the NER data bank (databank.nedfi.com).

### 6.4 Ministry of DoNER and NEC
- **NEC (North Eastern Council)**: statutory body under the **NEC Act, 1971**, came into being **7 Nov 1972**, **HQ Shillong**.
  - Since **June 2018**: **Union Home Minister = ex-officio Chairman**; **Minister of DoNER = ex-officio Vice-Chairman**.
  - After the **2002 amendment**, members are the Governors and CMs of the member states plus **3 members nominated by the President**. It became a regional planning body.
- **PM-DevINE** (PM's Development Initiative for NE Region): Cabinet approval **12 Oct 2022**, **₹6,600 cr** for **2022-23 to 2025-26**, run by MDoNER.
  - Aims: infrastructure in the spirit of PM GatiShakti, social development projects, youth and women livelihoods, filling development gaps.
  - FLAG: continuation into the 16th Finance Commission period (2026-31) not verified.
- Other DoNER schemes: NESIDS (North East Special Infrastructure Development Scheme) and "Schemes of NEC". Details not re-verified.

## 7. MSME essentials

### 7.1 Legal base and definitions
- **MSMED Act, 2006** is the governing law.
- Since **1 July 2020** there is one **composite criterion** for manufacturing and services: investment in P&M/equipment **AND** turnover (notification **S.O. 2119(E), 26 Jun 2020**). Exports are excluded from turnover.

| Category | 2020 limits (1 Jul 2020 – 31 Mar 2025): investment / turnover | **Current limits (from 1 Apr 2025)**: investment / turnover |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | ₹1 cr / ₹5 cr | **₹2.5 cr / ₹10 cr** |
| Small | ₹10 cr / ₹50 cr | **₹25 cr / ₹100 cr** |
| Medium | ₹50 cr / ₹250 cr | **₹125 cr / ₹500 cr** |

- Revision announced in the **Union Budget 2025-26 (1 Feb 2025)**: investment limits ×2.5, turnover limits ×2. Notified by **S.O. 1364(E), 21 Mar 2025**, **effective 1 Apr 2025**. No later revision found (as of Sep 2026).
- Exam trap: **MIIPP 2024 (§2.6) still quotes the 2020 limits** (₹1/5, 10/50, 50/250 cr) because it predates the revision.

### 7.2 Registration and credit
- **Udyam Registration**: rolled out **1 July 2020**. Free, paperless, based on self-declaration, and linked to PAN, GST and ITR data. It replaced Udyog Aadhaar (UAM).
- **Udyam Assist Platform (UAP)**: launched **11 Jan 2023** (with SIDBI) to bring **informal micro enterprises** into the formal sector for **priority-sector lending**. It had crossed 1.5 crore registrations by early 2024.
- **Budget 2025-26 credit measures**:
  - **CGTMSE** guarantee cover for Micro & Small raised from **₹5 cr to ₹10 cr** (effective 1 Apr 2025).
  - Startups: guarantee cover **₹10 cr → ₹20 cr**.
  - Well-run exporter MSMEs: term loans up to ₹20 cr.
  - **Customised credit cards with a ₹5 lakh limit** for Udyam-registered micro enterprises; 10 lakh cards in year 1.
  - New ₹10,000 cr Fund of Funds for startups.
  - **New scheme: loans up to ₹2 cr** for **5 lakh first-time women, SC and ST entrepreneurs** over 5 years.

FLAG: reports say the original scheme ended in Mar 2025 and a revamp (limits up to ₹2 cr) is under way. Regular startups: up to **10 years** from incorporation and turnover **≤ ₹200 cr** (earlier ₹100 cr). New **Deep Tech Startup** category: up to **20 years** and turnover **≤ ₹300 cr** |
| **MSME Sustainable (ZED) Certification** | Launched **28 Apr 2022** (then MSME Minister Narayan Rane). "Zero Defect, Zero Effect". Levels: **Bronze, Silver, Gold**. Subsidy on certification cost: **Micro 80%, Small 60%, Medium 50%**, plus **10% extra for women-, SC/ST-owned and NER MSMEs**. Up to ₹5 lakh per MSME for handholding. ₹10,000 joining reward for taking the ZED pledge |
| **DIC (District Industries Centre)** | Came from the **Industrial Policy Statement 1977**. Launched **1 May 1978** as a centrally sponsored programme. A **single-window at district level** for small, village and cottage industries: EDPs and training, project identification and feasibility reports, credit arrangement (PMEGP etc.), raw material and machinery linkage, cluster development, fairs and markets, and nodal role for licences and land allotment. In Meghalaya the equivalent is the **DCIC** |
| Other MSME facts | **MSE Public Procurement Policy (2012)**: 25% of central procurement from MSEs, including **4% from SC/ST** and **3% from women**-owned MSEs. **Delayed payments**: buyers must pay MSEs within **45 days** (MSMED Act §15; interest under §16; disputes to the MSE Facilitation Council/Samadhaan). **Income Tax §43B(h)** (from AY 2024-25): payments to MSEs made late are disallowed as a deduction. **MSME Day: 27 June**. *These items are standard facts; primary texts were not fetched in this session* |

### 7.4 National single window and investment facilitation
- **National Single Window System (NSWS)**: launched **22 Sep 2021** by Piyush Goyal.
- **FIPB abolished**: Cabinet decision **24 May 2017**. FDI approvals now go through the **Foreign Investment Facilitation Portal (FIFP)** to the concerned ministries, with DPIIT coordinating.

## 10. Industrial-economics terms for the IPO

### 10.1 GST
- **101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016**. GST rolled out on **1 July 2017**.
- Key articles:
  - **Art. 246A**: concurrent power of the Union and States to levy GST.
  - **Art. 269A**: IGST on inter-state supply.
  - **Art. 279A**: **GST Council**, chaired by the **Union Finance Minister**; State FMs are members.
- Components: **CGST + SGST/UTGST** on intra-state supplies; **IGST** on inter-state supplies and imports.
- **Input Tax Credit (ITC)**: tax paid on inputs is set off against output tax. "**Net GST**" = GST paid minus ITC. This is the base for SGST reimbursement under MIIPP and MSLI under UNNATI.
- **GST 2.0 / rate rationalisation**: **56th GST Council meeting, 3 Sep 2025**. From **22 Sep 2025**, mainly **two slabs: 5% and 18%**. The 12% and 28% slabs were largely merged into these. A **40% special rate** applies to sin/luxury goods (tobacco and pan masala are on a separate timeline).
- Registration threshold: **₹40 lakh (goods) / ₹20 lakh (services)** in normal states; **lower limits in special-category states**. FLAG: sources disagree on which limit Meghalaya applies (₹20 lakh or ₹40 lakh for goods), so check CBIC before quoting.
- **13 Aug 2026**: Meghalaya Cabinet approved a Bill amending the **Meghalaya GST Act, 2017** to match the national changes.

### 10.2 FDI
- Two routes:
  - **Automatic route**: no prior approval; the RBI is informed after the investment.
  - **Government route**: prior approval from the administrative ministry via the **FIFP**.
- Law: FEMA 1999 and the DPIIT consolidated FDI Policy.
- Prohibited sectors include lottery, gambling/betting, chit funds, Nidhi companies, trading in TDRs, real-estate business and farmhouses, tobacco cigars/cigarettes, atomic energy and some railway operations. *Standard fact; the DPIIT policy text was not re-fetched this session.*

### 10.3 Ease of doing business
- **BRAP (Business Reforms Action Plan)**: DPIIT's assessment of state reforms. Since BRAP 2020, states are placed in categories (Top Achievers, Achievers, Aspirers, **Emerging Business Ecosystems**) rather than ranked.
- **Meghalaya was an "Emerging Business Ecosystem" in BRAP 2020 and again in BRAP 2022.**
- The World Bank's *Doing Business* was discontinued in 2021 and replaced by **B-READY**.

### 10.4 SEZ and EPIP
- **SEZ Act, 2005**, with the SEZ Rules in force from **10 Feb 2006**. SEZs are duty-free enclaves treated as foreign territory for trade and duties.
- **EPIP (Export Promotion Industrial Park)**: a central scheme of the 1990s. Byrnihat's EPIP was implemented in 1996.
- FLAG: I did not find an operational SEZ in Meghalaya; not verified.

### 10.5 Monetary policy snapshot (for the "RBI repo rate" type question)
- **Repo rate 5.25%** (as of the MPC meeting of 3–5 Aug 2026; unchanged, neutral stance).
- **SDF 5.00%; MSF and Bank Rate 5.50%**.
- The last change was a cut from 5.50% to 5.25% in Dec 2025.
- Next MPC meeting: 5–7 Oct 2026, so re-check after that.

### 10.6 Other terms
- **Capital investment subsidy**: a one-time grant as a % of P&M.
- **Interest subvention**: part of the interest on a term loan reimbursed.
- **Margin money**: the promoter's share of the project cost, which banks require. Under PMEGP, the subsidy is kept as margin money.
- **Deemed approval**: a clearance treated as granted when the authority misses its deadline.
- **CAF**: the Combined Application Form for all clearances.
- **Negative list**: sectors excluded from incentives.
- **Local employment clause**: a domicile quota as a condition of incentives.
- **Composite criterion** (MSME): investment AND turnover.
- **Greenfield**: a new unit, as opposed to brownfield/expansion.

## 11. Interview one-liners (C&I Dept context)
- "What is MIIPP 2024?" The state industrial policy, notified 14 Mar 2024. It runs from 21 Dec 2022 to 20 Dec 2034 and is designed to top up UNNATI 2024. It offers a 30% capital incentive, 4–5% interest subvention for 7 years, 100% net-SGST reimbursement for 10–15 years, EPF, transport, green and power subsidies, with a ₹100 cr cap per unit. Units must meet local-employment quotas. Cement is on the negative list.
- "Who approves an investment proposal?" Under the MSIPF Act 2024: the District Investment Committee (DC) up to ₹1 cr; the State Investment Committee (Chief Secretary) for ₹1–10 cr; the High-Powered Committee (CM) above ₹10 cr and for customised packages above ₹100 cr. All through MIPA's Unified Investment Portal.
- "Why are only 3 Meghalaya districts in UNNATI Zone A?" Zones follow the NER District SDG Index. East Khasi Hills, West Jaintia Hills and West Garo Hills are Zone A; the other 9, including Ri-Bhoi/Byrnihat, are Zone B and get 50% capital incentive and 5% interest subvention.
- "Balancing industry and environment?" Byrnihat's air quality (IQAir 2024 worst in the world). The NGT's 2014 coal-mining ban and the shift to scientific mining. The 2026 anti-uranium resolution. Cement excluded from incentives. MIIPP's green subsidies.
- "Border trade?" 2 border haats (Kalaichar, Balat), suspended since Aug 2024; 4 more approved. 8 functional LCS. The Dawki ICP opened 5 May 2023.
